Purpose

The Enterprise Portfolio Generator serves as the foundational module of the framework.

The objective of this module is to create a realistic synthetic banking portfolio that supports downstream credit risk analytics, stress testing, regulatory reporting, executive dashboarding, and benchmarking.

All subsequent modules consume outputs generated by this module.

Business Objective

Banking institutions maintain large credit portfolios containing retail and wholesale lending exposures.

To simulate enterprise-scale risk analytics, this module generates:

Retail Banking Portfolio
Wholesale Banking Portfolio
Enterprise Banking Portfolio

The generated portfolio becomes the primary source dataset for:

Risk Segmentation
PD Analytics
LGD Analytics
EAD Analytics
