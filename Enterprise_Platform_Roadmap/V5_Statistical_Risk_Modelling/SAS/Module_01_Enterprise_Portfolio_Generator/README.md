PURPOSE
-------

The Enterprise Portfolio Generator is the foundational module of the
Enterprise Risk Analytics & Regulatory Reporting Framework.

The module creates a realistic synthetic enterprise banking portfolio
used by all downstream risk analytics modules.

The generated portfolio supports:

- Risk Segmentation
- PD Analytics
- LGD Analytics
- EAD Analytics
- ECL Calculation
- Stress Testing
- Executive Dashboard Reporting
- Benchmarking

==============================================================================

BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
------------------

Banking institutions manage large portfolios of retail and wholesale
credit exposures.

This module generates a synthetic enterprise-scale banking portfolio
for risk analytics and regulatory reporting purposes.

Generated Portfolio Components

- Retail Portfolio
- Wholesale Portfolio
- Enterprise Portfolio

==============================================================================

PORTFOLIO SPECIFICATIONS

Retail Accounts      : 100,000

Wholesale Accounts   : 100,000

Total Portfolio      : 200,000

INPUT REQUIREMENTS
